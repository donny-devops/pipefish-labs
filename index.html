The Quantum Threat

Q-Day Isn't Coming.
It's Scheduled.

When a sufficiently powerful quantum computer comes online — and it will — every RSA key, every elliptic curve signature, every TLS handshake becomes retroactively breakable. "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" attacks are already underway. Your encrypted data from 2024 may be decrypted in 2031. You can't go back and re-encrypt it.

NIST finalized its first PQC standards in 2024. Federal mandates are active. Your competitors are starting to migrate. PipeFish Labs has been building quantum-ready infrastructure since before the standards were finalized — and we're ready to move yours.

⚠️

Every day of delay is another day of exposure you can't walk back. The cryptographic attack surface you have today is exactly the attack surface an adversary will exploit the moment quantum capability crosses the threshold.
