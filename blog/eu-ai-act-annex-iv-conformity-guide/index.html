Navigating EU AI Act Annex IV Conformity & Immutable Audit Trails

By Jason F., Founder & Chief Information Security Officer • Published August 5, 2026 • 7 min read

The enforcement of the EU AI Act mandates strict technical compliance for high-risk AI deployments, including financial credit assessment, insurance underwriting, and healthcare intake systems. Annex IV outlines mandatory technical documentation that must be generated automatically during system operation.

1. Immutable Log Generation

Every decision executed by a PipeFish agent chain emits an append-only JSON event to an immutable audit store. Each event records the input prompt, model version, system prompt hash, temperature parameter, and output confidence score.

2. Human-in-the-Loop Override Mechanisms

Annex IV mandates that operators retain real-time override authority. Our orchestration framework embeds fallback latches allowing human supervisors to halt, adjust, or manually approve agent decisions in real time.

3. Automated Conformity Assessments

We provide enterprise clients with continuous AI Act readiness dashboards, automated Annex IV technical dossiers, and quarterly bias audit reports ready for European Notified Body inspection.
