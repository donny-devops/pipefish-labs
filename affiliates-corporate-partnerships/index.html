Common Questions

Partner FAQ

How long does it take to start earning commissions?
Most affiliates earn their first commission within 30–60 days of their first qualified referral. The sales cycle for our Growth tier averages 3 weeks from first call to signed contract. Enterprise deals take longer but pay proportionally more.
Is there a minimum commitment to join the affiliate program?
No minimum commitment for affiliates. There's no fee to join and no minimum referral volume. If you go 12 months without a qualified referral, your account moves to inactive (easily reactivated with one email to your partner manager).
Can I be both an affiliate and a client simultaneously?
Absolutely — and we actively encourage it. Clients who are also affiliates are our most effective referrers because they speak from personal experience. There is no conflict of interest, and your referral commissions are independent of your client contract.
What does the white-label option actually include?
White-label resellers get: custom-branded client portal with your domain, NDA-protected delivery (we never contact your client directly without your permission), your branding on all reports and dashboards, and the option for us to ghost-deliver where you present results as your own work. We require an NDA and minimum $25K/mo volume commitment.
Are referral commissions recurring or one-time?
Referral commissions are recurring — you earn 20% of contract value every month your referral remains a client. The commission is based on the base contract value (not overages or add-ons). There's no time cap: if a client you referred stays 3 years, you earn for 3 years.
