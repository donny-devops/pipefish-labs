Common questions

Partner FAQ

How is referral commission calculated?
Commission is based on the first-year contract value of referred clients who sign a service agreement. Payment is made within 30 days of the client's first invoice being paid.
Is there an exclusivity requirement?
No. PipeFish partnerships are non-exclusive. You are free to refer to or partner with other vendors in adjacent spaces. We do ask that you do not actively refer clients to direct competitors.
What does onboarding look like for new partners?
New partners complete a 60-minute onboarding call, receive access to the partner portal and co-branded materials, and are introduced to their dedicated partner success manager. The process takes less than one week.
Who qualifies for the technology partnership track?
Any SaaS platform, API provider, or software vendor whose product is commonly used by freight logistics, legal, or operations teams. Common examples: TMS providers, CRM platforms, practice management software, document automation tools.
